## 3. Synthetic Date Elimination

Fabricating dates to trigger freshness signals violates search quality guidelines and distorts indexation:

1. **Project Case Studies (`TechArticle`):**
   - All arbitrary `datePublished: "2024-01-01"` or `project.year + "-01-01"` patterns have been eliminated from `getProjectCaseStudySchema()` in `lib/schema.ts`.
   - `datePublished` and `dateModified` are cleanly omitted because exact day-level publication timestamps are not tracked per project.
2. **Blog Detail (`Article`):**
   - The hardcoded `"2026-01-15T00:00:00+05:30"` fallback was removed from `app/blog/[slug]/page.tsx`. `datePublished` is dynamically sourced only when `note.date` is explicitly defined.
3. **Sitemap (`sitemap.xml`):**
   - `lastModified` timestamps are deliberately omitted from `app/sitemap.ts` to prevent search engines from receiving blanket fabricated update dates, allowing crawlers to rely on genuine HTTP `Last-Modified` / `ETag` headers.

## 4. Google AI Search & Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

### Official Google AI Search Guidance
According to Google Search Central's official documentation regarding Google AI Overviews and Gemini-powered search:
1. **No Proprietary AI Schema or `llms.txt` Needed:**  
   Google AI Search features rely strictly on standard web indexability (Googlebot), clean semantic HTML, and standard Schema.org structured data. Google does **not** require or recognize proprietary files like `llms.txt` for AI indexing.
2. **Entity Disambiguation via Knowledge Graph:**  
   By establishing a canonical `@id` (`https://jayant.codes/#jayant`) for the `Person` entity and cross-referencing it in `publisher`, `author`, `creator`, and `mainEntity`, AI retrieval models can unambiguously resolve Jayant Potdar as an individual entity distinct from others.
3. **First-Person Experience (E-E-A-T):**  
   Google AI Overviews strongly prioritize first-person authentic technical experience. Case studies feature detailed architectural trade-offs, limitations, concrete benchmarks, and verifiable source code repositories.
4. **Crawl Access in `app/robots.ts`:**  
   Explicit crawl rules permit both traditional search engines and AI search/citation bots:
   - `Googlebot`, `Bingbot`, `Applebot` (Core Web Search)
   - `OAI-SearchBot`, `PerplexityBot` (Real-time AI Answer Engines)
   - `Google-Extended`, `GPTBot`, `ClaudeBot` (AI Training and Synthesis)
